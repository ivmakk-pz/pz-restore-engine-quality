title=Restore Engine Quality [B42]
description=Restore your vehicle's engine quality with a Wrench, Spare Engine Parts, and Mechanical skill 4+
description=
description=Unlike vanilla engine [i]condition[/i] (which affects durability), engine [i]quality[/i] determines starting reliability, power output, and cold weather performance. This mod lets you restore quality from any percentage up to 100%.
description=
description=[h2]Key Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Engine Quality Restoration[/b]: Improve quality from any percentage up to 100%
description=[*][b]Skill-Based Mechanics[/b]: Higher Mechanics skill = better quality increases per iteration
description=[*][b]Configurable Resource Cost[/b]: Adjust engine parts consumption multiplier in Menu -> Options -> Mods -> Restore Engine Quality (1-5 per restoration, default: 2)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]How to Use[/h2]
description=[b]Requirements:[/b] Wrench, Spare Engine Parts, appropriate Mechanics skill level (4+)
description=[b]Access:[/b] Right-click vehicle Engine ? Restore Engine Quality [i](the same context menu where "Repair Engine" located)[/i]
description=[b]Process:[/b] Each restoration consumes engine parts and improves quality based on your skill
description=
description=[h2]Resource Cost[/h2]
description=By default, requires 2x more Spare Engine Parts than regular engine repair (making it more challenging but not grindy). You can adjust this multiplier in mod options from 1x (same as repair) to 5x (very expensive).
description=
description=[h2]Engine Quality Effects[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Starting Reliability[/b]: Lower quality = higher chance to fail starting
description=[*][b]Engine Power[/b]: Higher quality = more horsepower and performance
description=[*][b]Cold Weather Starting[/b]: Quality ?65% struggles in cold weather
description=[/list]
description=
