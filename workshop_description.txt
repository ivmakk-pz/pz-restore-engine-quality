[b]Restore Engine Quality[/b] - A comprehensive mod that allows players to restore and maintain vehicle engine quality in Project Zomboid.

This mod provides mechanics to repair degraded vehicle engines and maintain optimal performance throughout your survival experience.

[*]Realistic engine maintenance features
[/list]
